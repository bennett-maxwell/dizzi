Key Facts

$50,000

Traditional franchise, paid to USVI Dizzibrands, LLC on signing. Limited Service fee not separately stated. Refund only if terminated before opening, less expenses. (FDD Item 5, p.3-4)

Estimated Initial Investment

Royalty

4% of Gross Revenues

Paid weekly by Wednesday for the preceding week. Limited Service: minimum $6,000/year Continuing Fee. (FDD Item 6, p.5)

Brand Fund

1% of Gross Revenues

Marketing and Promotion Fund, administered and controlled by Dizzi. (FDD Item 6, p.5)

Local Advertising

1.5% + 0.5% to DMA Fund

Paid quarterly to local suppliers and the DMA fund; quarterly reports to Dizzi within 10 days. (FDD Item 6, p.5)

Transfer Fee

$12,500

Includes training of the transferee franchisee and team. (FDD Item 6, p.5)

Territory

Exclusive — 1-mile radius

From the Franchised Location; approved Delivery Area sales allowed. No right to additional franchises. Dizzi may open other restaurants outside the area. (FDD Item 12, p.20)

Term

10 years (Traditional) / 5 years (Limited Service)

Renewal on Dizzi's then-current standard terms. Post-term non-compete: 9 months, 5-mile radius. (FDD Item 17, p.23)

Liquor License

Required — termination risk

Franchise can be terminated if the liquor/food license is not obtained; $50,000 fee deduction if denied for criminal history/bankruptcy. (FDD Item 5, p.4)

Training

Classroom + on-the-job

Classroom at Dizzi offices in St Thomas, USVI; on-the-job at an operational Dizzi restaurant. Must open within 170 days of signing. (FDD Item 11, p.13)

Litigation

None disclosed

Item 3: no litigation required to be disclosed. Item 4: no bankruptcy filings. (FDD Items 3-4, p.3)

Item 19 — Financial Performance

No representations

Dizzi makes no representations about future or past financial performance of any outlets. (FDD Item 19, p.27)

Risk Profile and Disclosure

38 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate-low. The dominant risk inputs are informational rather than financial: the FDD makes no financial performance representations (Item 19) and discloses no outlet or franchisee list (Item 20), which limits a buyer's ability to verify unit economics or contact existing franchisees before purchasing. Offsetting this are zero disclosed litigation matters, an exclusive 1-mile territory, and modest royalty and brand-fund rates of 4% and 1% of gross revenues. The alcohol-service licensing dependency is the sharpest operational risk: the franchisor may terminate the agreement if the required liquor license is not obtained, and a denial tied to criminal history or bankruptcy status triggers a $50,000 deduction from any initial-fee refund.

  • −8 — Exclusive 1-mile territory (Item 12)
  • −4 — Modest fees: 4% royalty, 1% brand fund
